Seed Formats: BIP39, SLIP-39 & Multisig

Understand the three main approaches to seed backup and recovery.

Feature BIP39 SLIP-39 Multisig
Structure Single 12-24 word phrase Multiple shares (2-of-3, 3-of-5) Multiple independent seeds
Single Point of Failure Yes - one loss = no recovery No - requires N shares to recover No - requires M of N keys
Theft Risk One backup stolen = all funds at risk Attacker needs multiple shares Attacker needs multiple keys
Wallet Support Universal - all wallets Limited support Most hardware wallets
Best For Standard backups, most users High-security setups Large holdings, institutions

Backup Best Practices

✓ Multi-Backup Strategy:
  • Generate seed offline using verified tools on air-gapped computer
  • Create multiple physical backups using different methods (metal + paper + steganography)
  • Store backups in geographically diverse locations
  • Keep seed and passphrase completely separate in different locations
  • Test recovery before funding your wallet
  • Use redundancy—no single backup method is perfect
✓ Where to Store Backups:
  • Home Safe: Fireproof safe bolted to floor. Accessible but protected from fire/theft
  • Bank Safety Deposit Box: Secure, climate-controlled, but requires bank cooperation in emergency
  • Trusted Family Member: Someone you trust completely (parent, sibling). Different geographic location
  • Lawyer or Executor: Can hold sealed backup with instructions for inheritance/emergency access
  • Multiple Locations: Split backups across 2-3 locations so no single theft/disaster loses everything
  • Geographic Diversity: Different cities or countries to protect against regional disasters
⚠️ NEVER Do These:
  • Never: Type your seed into ANY internet-connected device
  • Never: Store seed in digital files, email, cloud storage, or photos
  • Never: Tell anyone your complete seed phrase (split it if sharing)
  • Never: Screenshot or photograph your seed phrase
  • Never: Write recovery info on sticky notes or loose paper
  • Never: Store seed + passphrase in same location
  • Never: Rely on a single backup method (always use 2+)
  • Never: Leave seed phrase visible during wallet setup
  • Never: Carry your seed phrase with you in wallet or bag
✓ Sharing With Trusted Loved Ones:
  • Inheritance Planning: Leave instructions for spouse/executor on how to access recovery backups
  • Split Responsibility: Give different parts of seed to different trusted people (e.g., words 1-8 to one person, 9-12 to another)
  • SLIP-39 Shares: Use threshold shares (2-of-3) where 3 trusted people each hold 1 share, but only 2 needed to recover
  • Emergency Access Only: Brief trusted family member on backup location/access in case of death/emergency
  • Document Instructions: Leave clear written instructions on how to use recovery backup (which wallet, which tool, what to do)
  • Test Together (Optional): Test recovery with trusted person on testnet to verify backup works
  • Keep it Secret: Even trusted people should not know the full seed—just location or access method
  • Regular Review: Update your loved ones if backup locations change or strategy evolves
